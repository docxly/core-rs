# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

`docxly` is a technical project, but collaboration quality still matters. We expect contributors and maintainers to communicate with respect, argue from evidence, and keep discussion focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- be direct and constructive
- assume good intent until there is evidence otherwise
- discuss code and behavior, not personal traits
- provide technical context when challenging a decision
- respect the time and boundaries of other contributors

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, abuse, or intimidation
- discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- repeated bad-faith argument after maintainers have made a decision
- publishing private information without permission
- derailing technical discussions into personal conflict

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that undermine a safe and productive working environment.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns that should not be handled in a public issue or pull request, contact a maintainer directly through GitHub.

If the report is about a maintainer, include enough context for another maintainer to review the situation independently.
